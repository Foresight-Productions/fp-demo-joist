Straight answers, from the state's program rules and how JOIST works.

  1. What is the Clean Buildings Performance Standard?

    Washington's Clean Buildings Performance Standard (CBPS) is the state law, passed in 2019 and expanded in 2022, that requires owners of buildings over 20,000 square feet to track and manage their energy use. For Tier 2 buildings, that means benchmarking in ENERGY STAR Portfolio Manager, an operations and maintenance program, and an Energy Management Plan. Unlike Tier 1, Tier 2 buildings don't have to meet an energy performance target.

  2. Does my building have to comply?

    If it's over 20,000 square feet, probably. Tier 2 covers buildings from 20,000 to 50,000 square feet, plus all multifamily buildings over 20,000. Tier 1 covers buildings over 50,000. Manufacturing, agricultural, vacant and semi-heated buildings may qualify for an exemption.

  3. How can this cost me nothing?

    Qualifying Tier 2 buildings earn an Early Adopter Incentive of $0.30 per square foot, paid to the owner by their electric or gas utility. JOIST's Tier 2 pricing is matched to that incentive, and you forward our fee within 30 days of receiving it.

  4. When is the deadline?

    Tier 2 buildings must submit compliance and incentive applications by July 1, 2027. Incentive money is first come, first served, so filing earlier matters.

  5. How long does it take?

    The site visit and paperwork usually take 1 to 3 months. After the state approves your package, the utility payment typically arrives within several weeks.

  6. What happens if I miss the deadline?

    The Department of Commerce can assess progressive penalties, up to $0.30 per square foot. You'd also lose your place in line for incentive money.
