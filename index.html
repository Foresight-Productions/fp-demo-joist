    Straight answers

    Still unsure? Call or text Jacob at (509) 379-8956.

    Does my building have to comply?

    If it's over 20,000 square feet, probably. Tier 2 covers buildings from 20,000 to 50,000 square feet plus all multifamily buildings over 20,000. Tier 1 covers buildings over 50,000. Manufacturing, agricultural, vacant and semi-heated buildings may qualify for exemptions.

    When is the deadline?

    Tier 2 buildings must submit compliance and incentive applications by July 1, 2027. Incentive money is first come, first served, so filing earlier matters.

    How can this cost me nothing?

    Qualifying Tier 2 buildings earn an Early Adopter Incentive of $0.30 per square foot, paid directly to the owner by their electric or gas utility. JOIST's Tier 2 pricing is matched to that incentive, and you forward payment to us within 30 days of receiving it.

    How long does it take?

    The site visit and paperwork usually take 1 to 3 months. After the state approves your package, the utility payment typically arrives within several weeks.

    What happens if I miss the deadline?

    The Department of Commerce can assess progressive penalties for non-compliance, up to $0.30 per square foot. You'd also lose your place in line for incentive money.
